```
CALCULATE EXACTLY:

1. REVENUE AT RISK CALCULATION:
   Formula: (affected_customers × avg_arr) × churn_rate
   
   Example with actual numbers:
   - Affected customers: 200 (from required_field_added)
   - Avg customer ARR: $20,000
   - Historical churn: 2.5%
   - Base revenue risk: 200 × $20,000 = $4,000,000 at risk
   - Expected churn: 2.5% × $4,000,000 = $100,000 direct loss
   - Lifetime value loss: $100,000 × 3 years = $300,000 total
   
   Show this calculation step-by-step with actual numbers.
   Then provide: best-case (1% churn), worst-case (5% churn), likely-case (2.5% churn)

2. SUPPORT COST OVERHEAD CALCULATION:
   Formula: (total_breaking_changes × expected_support_tickets) × avg_ticket_cost + indirect_costs
   
   Example with actual numbers:
   - 2 breaking changes
   - Expect 2-3 support tickets per affected customer
   - 200 affected × 2.5 tickets = 500 tickets
   - Cost per ticket: $150
   - Direct: 500 × $150 = $75,000
   
   Indirect costs to include:
   - Developer debugging time: Estimate 50 hours × $250/hr = $12,500
   - Customer success escalations: Estimate 30 hours × $200/hr = $6,000
   - Emergency hotfixes (bugs in migration): Estimate 20 hours × $300/hr = $6,000
   - Engineering time writing migration guides: Estimate 40 hours × $200/hr = $8,000
   - Lost developer productivity (context switching): Estimate 100 hours × $150/hr = $15,000
   
   Total indirect: $47,500
   Total support cost: $75,000 + $47,500 = $122,500
   
   Show EXACT breakdown. Don't round.

3. ENTERPRISE CUSTOMER RISK:
   Formula: (enterprise_customers × enterprise_arr) × higher_churn_rate
   
   Example with actual numbers:
   - 45 enterprise customers using affected endpoints
   - Avg enterprise ARR: $100,000
   - Enterprise churn on breaking changes: 4% (higher than SMB 2%)
   - Revenue at risk: 45 × $100,000 = $4,500,000
   - Expected churn: 4% × $4,500,000 = $180,000
   - Reason: Enterprises demand stability, have alternatives, negotiate hard
   
   Show this as separate line item (enterprises are different risk profile).

4. REPUTATION/RETENTION RISK:
   Formula: (negative_reviews × customer_acquisition_cost) + (reduced_signups × avg_cust_value)
   
   Example with actual numbers:
   - 15% of affected customers leave negative reviews on G2/Capterra
   - 200 affected × 15% = 30 negative reviews
   - Negative review impact: Reduces new customer signups by 10%
   - Assume 50 new customers/month × 12 = 600 new/year
   - Lost signups: 600 × 10% = 60 customers

   - Reputation loss: 60 × $60,000 = $3,600,000 (first year impact)
   - Realistic: 5% lost signups (more conservative) = $1,800,000
   
   Show reasoning for percentage used.

5. OPPORTUNITY COST:
   Formula: (engineering_hours + support_hours + sales_impact) × hourly_rate
   
   Example with actual numbers:
   - Engineering building migration toolkit: 40 hours × $200/hr = $8,000
   - DevRel content creation: 30 hours × $200/hr = $6,000
   - Customer success managing transitions: 100 hours × $150/hr = $15,000

   This is cost of managing the release, not the breaking changes themselves.

TOTAL LIABILITY = Revenue Risk + Support Cost + Enterprise Risk + Reputation Risk + Opportunity Cost

Example: $300K + $122.5K + $180K + $1.8M + $43K = $2,445,500

- Which customers are most at risk?
- What can they do RIGHT NOW to reduce cost?
- Timeline: When should they release to minimize impact?
- Comparison: How does this compare to Stripe/Shopify releases?
```

```
The user has breaking API changes costing $[total_liability] to fix.

3. Include exact steps to implement
4. Show ROI (cost to implement vs savings)

STRATEGIES TO CONSIDER:

1. AUTO-GENERATED MIGRATION GUIDES
   - How: Use your diff engine to auto-generate "Before/After" code for each breaking change
   - Time: 8 hours to build
   - Saves: ~$30K in support costs (customers self-service instead of tickets)
   - ROI: $30K saved / 8 hours = $3,750/hour ROI
   
   Ask: Should they do this? Show the math.

2. AUTOMATED SDK GENERATORS
   - How: Auto-generate SDK updates for Python, JS, Go that handle v1→v2 migration
   - Time: 16 hours to build
   - Saves: ~$50K in support (SDKs do the heavy lifting)
   - ROI: $50K / 16 hours = $3,125/hour ROI
   
   Ask: Is this worth the engineering time?

3. 1-ON-1 MIGRATION SUPPORT FOR TOP CUSTOMERS
   - How: Assign engineering time to hand-hold top 10 enterprise customers
   - Time: 40 hours support
   - Cost: 40 × $200/hr = $8,000
   - Saves: $50K in enterprise churn (they stay because of white-glove service)
   - ROI: $50K saved / $8K cost = 6.25x ROI
   
   Ask: Should they do this for top customers?

4. EXTENDED DEPRECATION PERIOD (90 days instead of 30)
   - How: Keep both v1 and v2 running in parallel for 90 days
   - Time: 10 hours (engineering coordination)
   - Cost: Infrastructure $2K/month × 3 months = $6K
   - Saves: $100K in churn (customers have time to migrate)
   - ROI: $100K saved / $6K cost = 16.6x ROI
   
   Ask: This is the best ROI. Should they definitely do this?

5. AUTOMATED BREAKING CHANGE DETECTION (GITHUB ACTIONS BOT)
   - How: Catch breaking changes in future PRs before they happen
   - Time: 12 hours to build
   - Saves: Prevents accidental future breaks = saves $847K+ per incident
   - ROI: Prevents huge future costs, priceless
   
   Ask: Should they build this bot?

For each strategy:
- Show exact cost to implement
- Show exact savings
- Show ROI
- Give a clear yes/no recommendation

Then, create a PRIORITIZED MITIGATION PLAN:
1. Do these first (highest ROI)
2. Do these second (medium ROI)
3. Do these only if budget allows (nice-to-have)
```
